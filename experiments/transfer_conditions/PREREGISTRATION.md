# Transfer-conditions study: pre-registration

Registered 2026-10-26, before any model was trained or evaluated on the data below.
Code: `experiments/transfer_conditions/src/`. Nothing in `experiments/fedultra_fresh/` or `ultra/` is modified.

## Why this study exists

The audit found that federated ULTRA cannot show transfer on Enterprise↔ICS at full data,
and that the cause is the data, not the model:

1. ICS has enough data to learn its own patterns (local fine-tuning converges; pooling rule statistics adds ≈0).
2. `targets`, 56% of ICS validation queries, has no Enterprise counterpart.
3. What the clients' updates share is mostly score calibration (head cosine 0.73, relation model 0.04).
4. The knowledge ICS lacks lives in entity identity, which ULTRA does not see.

Transfer through shared weights needs three data conditions: the target is short of
supervision (**need**), the source holds the same pattern (**availability**), and the relations
play the same structural roles in both graphs (**channel**). This study creates those
conditions on purpose and tests whether federated ULTRA then transfers.

**What this study can and cannot show.**
- **Study S** is synthetic. It shows when federated ULTRA transfers multi-hop patterns between
  clients that share no entities and no relation IDs. It is not evidence about ATT&CK.
- **Study R** uses only real ATT&CK facts and simulates a data-poor organisation. It is the real-data claim.
- The original Enterprise↔ICS (full data) result stands whatever this study finds.

## Shared protocol (both studies)

- Start from `ckpts/ultra_4g.pth` (same as all earlier runs).
- Arms:
  - **local:** target-only fine-tuning.
  - **fedavg:** target + source, equal client weights.
- Budget for both arms: 20 rounds × 10 local steps (200 target updates), batch 8, 64 negatives,
  AdamW lr 5e-4, weight decay 0. FedAvg resets AdamW each round; local keeps its AdamW state
  (the conventions of `fedavg_runner.py`).
- The target uses the same sampling stream in both arms.
- Selection: the round (0 = pretrained) with the best target validation MRR. Target test is
  scored once, for that round only.
- Metric: filtered MRR over all target entities, head and tail queries. Filters include
  `hidden.txt` (true facts removed by the scarcity protocol). Hidden facts never enter training
  or message passing.
- Δ = FedAvg − local target test MRR, paired by seed (`compare_arms.py`).
- **Full success criterion** (unchanged from the project): mean Δ ≥ +0.03, seed-level 95% t-CI
  entirely above 0, and hierarchical paired bootstrap 95% CI entirely above 0
  (`fedultra_fresh/src/bootstrap_cyber_transfer.py`, 20,000 samples, seed 20261026).
- All registered cells are reported whatever their outcome.

## Study S — synthetic clients with planted multi-hop attack patterns

Data: `synth_attack_kg.py`, generator seed 20261101 (manifest in `data/synthetic/manifest.json`).

**Schema.** One schema for every client:
- employs (G→S), implements (S→T), attributed-to (C→G), campaign-uses (C→T)
- runs-on (S→A), targets (T→A), mitigates (M→T), subtechnique-of (T→T)
- uses (G→T): the evaluated relation

**Generation.**
- `uses` is generated by the client's attack grammar:
  - *toolkit*: uses(G,T) ← employs(G,S) ∧ implements(S,T)
  - *campaign*: uses(G,T) ← attributed-to(C,G) ∧ campaign-uses(C,T)
- Each grammar-reachable technique is used with p = 0.6, plus Poisson(1) random techniques per group.
- Background relations are drawn independently of the grammar.
- Sub-techniques inherit their parent's mitigations and assets in every client.

**Target.**
- 80 groups, toolkit grammar.
- `uses` facts: 136 validation, 272 test, and a training pool of 408.
- Four scarcity levels n ∈ {13, 41, 136, 408} observed training facts. The levels are nested;
  the remaining pool facts are hidden.

**Sources.** 150 groups, one shared background, two grammars:
- `source_toolkit` (**shared** pattern)
- `source_campaign` (**disjoint** pattern)

All source facts are training facts.

**Evaluation.** Target test `uses` facts only.

**Seeds.** 20261111–20261115 (5 training seeds per cell; 8 cells = 2 sources × 4 levels).
Local runs are shared by both sources.

**Hypotheses.**
- **H1 (primary).** Shared source, n = 13: Δ meets the full success criterion.
- **H2.** Shared source: Δ(n = 13) > Δ(n = 408); the gain shrinks as the target gets more data.
- **H3.** Δ_shared − Δ_disjoint > 0 at every n, and Δ_disjoint ≤ 0 at n = 408 (negative transfer
  when the patterns differ).

**Sanity checks** (validation only and non-comparative, run before any FedAvg run):
- **S1.** Pretrained zero-shot target validation MRR at n = 408 is ≤ 0.60, so the task is not
  already solved by the pretrained prior.
- **S2.** The local arm at n = 408 (seed 20261111, `--skip-test`) reaches validation MRR
  ≥ zero-shot + 0.05 within the budget, so the planted pattern is learnable by ULTRA.
- **S3.** The planted path relations are distinguishable by ULTRA for query `uses`, and relation
  roles align across clients (`relation_roles.py`). **Passed at registration.** Only
  subtechnique-of and its inverse share a colour, and same-name pretrained cosine is 1.000 across
  all clients.

If S1 or S2 fails, the generator's sizes, p, or the budget may be changed once, logged as a
deviation, and the sanity checks rerun. FedAvg is not run until they pass. FedAvg results
never feed back into the generator.

## Study R — real ATT&CK facts, data-poor target

Data: `build_real_targets.py` (manifest in `data/real/manifest.json`).

**Clients.**
- Source: Enterprise, the frozen `fedultra_fresh` training split.
- Confirmatory target: **Mobile ATT&CK v19.2**, split with the fresh builder (same relation set,
  split seed 20261023, the 4 triples identical to Enterprise removed). 1,495 train, 170
  validation, 166 test. No model has been evaluated on Mobile before this study.
- Development target: **ICS** frozen split, scored on validation only (`--skip-test`). The ICS
  test set is spent and is not scored.

**Scarcity.** Fractions f ∈ {0.10, 0.25, 1.00} of target training facts, per relation:
max(1, round(f·n_r)), nested, subsample seed 20261102. Removed facts are hidden; validation and
test are unchanged.

**Seeds.**
- Mobile: 20261121–20261130 (10).
- ICS development: 20261141–20261143 (3).

**Hypotheses.**
- **H4 (primary, real data).** Mobile, f = 0.25: Δ meets the full success criterion.
  f = 0.25 was chosen before any model run, because the validation-only rule analysis in the
  audit showed the largest pooled gain at 25% of ICS data.
- **Secondary, reported with the same statistics and labelled secondary:**
  - Mobile at f = 0.10 and f = 1.00.
  - ICS validation at all three fractions (descriptive only; selected-round validation MRR is
    optimistic for both arms).
